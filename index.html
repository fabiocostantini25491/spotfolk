How it works

Fabio Costa Ph is three things: a portfolio of my photographs, free tools to plan the light, and SPOTFOLK — the travel community where members share and sign the places worth the trip. Here's how to get the most out of it.

01

Explore

An interactive map of my journeys. Every astronaut is a place I've photographed.

  • Tap an astronaut to open the place: photos, notes and the story behind it.
  • Use the search (magnifier, top right) to find places, photos and articles.
02

Gallery

All my photographs, filterable by category.

  • Tap a photo to open it full screen. On a phone, turn it sideways or use the rotate button.
  • Tap the heart on the photos you like to build a selection, then send it to me in one message.
  • Every photo is available for licensing (editorial and commercial) and as a fine-art print.
03

Field Notes

The travel magazine of the site, written by me and by the SpotFolk folk: practical stories, not poetry — when to go, where to park, how long the walk is, what settings to use and the mistakes to avoid.

  • All stories — every article, filtered by topic: reportage, places, current affairs, photography and technique, practical guides.
  • This month's issue — the magazine builds itself: every month the published stories become an issue of Field Notes From Planet Earth, with its cover, to read on the site or download as a PDF.
  • Guides — places zone by zone: what to see, when to go, where to stop, with a printable PDF and a PDF map that links every point to Google Maps.
  • Every folk can write — tap ✍ Write for Field Notes, send your story with your photos, and once it's published you become a co-author of the magazine: your byline carries your name and your folk, and the story goes into that month's issue.
  • Folk of the month — every issue tells the story of one member of the community.
04

Tools

Free tools to plan a shoot, wherever you are or wherever you're going.

  • Sun & Moon — search a place or tap Near me, pick a date: sunrise and sunset, golden and blue hour, twilight, moon phase and the Milky Way core.
  • Planner — a 3D map with the real terrain: see where the sun, the moon and the Milky Way rise and set behind the mountains. Drag to rotate, move the time and follow them across the sky.
  • Analyze your shot — load a test shot and get a shooting plan: exposure, maximum shutter speed for sharp stars, bracketing and stacking. The photo never leaves your device.
05

SpotFolk

The travel toolkit built by travellers: 8,000+ photo spots and points of interest scouted with a photographer's eye (many from research for future trips, waiting for someone to confirm them), places to eat and sleep, trails, trips and Pass it on — added, signed and reviewed by the community.

Visitors and members

  • Everyone sees a free preview: one place every ~15 km, each with its full card — exact location, the folk who signed it, stories and photos.
  • Members see the whole map — every place with its exact location, photos, stories, real prices and live alerts — and can sign, share and add their own.
  • Joining is free: go to fabiocostaph.com/#join, enter your name, email and a password. You're in straight away.

Finding places

  • Search a spot or a town, or tap Near me and choose the radius (1–50 km).
  • Filter by category, show or hide places with alerts, or only Support local.

Folk and signatures

  • Choose your folk when you join (you can pick more than one): Photo, Hiker, Backpacker, Road trip, Nomad, Foodie. Change them any time from My profile.
  • Sign a place you've been to and recommend: “✍ Sign as hiker folk”, with a one-line tip if you like. Each place shows who signed it (“📷 12 · 🥾 4”), and reviews show the rating of each folk.
  • ★ Fabio's pick marks the places I recommend most. Members who sign many places in one area can become Local experts.

Trips and Pass it on

  • Trips (in Community): tap the map, or right-click on a computer, for every stop, and say how you got there — on foot, scooter, train, boat. Start and arrival show on the map and on the globe.
  • Pass it on: leaving and can't take something home? Sell it or give it away to the travellers arriving after you. Deals are only between members: SpotFolk takes no payments.

Adding and updating

  • Add a place: only category, name and coordinates are required. Photos, description and community notes are optional.
  • Workshops: add dates, places in total, booked so far, price, a link and any special offer. Update the bookings any time from the workshop's card. It disappears from the map on the day it starts.
  • Trekking: put the pin on the starting point of the trail, so treks are easy to tell apart from viewpoints. Add difficulty (T, E, EE, EEA), time, length, elevation gain and where to park: the average slope is worked out for you.
  • On any place you can add photos, write a review (with what you paid for food and stays) or report a problem.

Categories

  • Photo spots
  • Food & drink
  • Places to stay
  • Workshops
  • Trekking

Signs on the map

  • Red dot — live alert from members: closed, works or landslide, event, very crowded, danger.
  • Green ring — Support local: villages and small businesses that live on visitors.
  • Glowing leaf — new in Support local (first month).
  • Glowing icon — new place or workshop (first 7 days). New and special places stay visible even when you zoom out.
  • %Glowing with % — workshop with a special offer.
  • Plane — workshop almost full: fewer than 3 places left.
  • 🥾Folk badge and ring — signed by the folk: the badge shows the folk who signed it most; a coloured ring from 5 signatures, a glowing double ring from 15. ★ = Fabio's pick
  • Dashed border — Fragile place: leave no trace, no geotags.

Many spots are places I've saved but not visited myself: always check access, rules and conditions before you go. Read the full notes below the map.

  • Messages — what you write to me by email, in the feedback form or in a request to become a contributor.
