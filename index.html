    Our code, in seven lines
    1. Leave no trace. Take only photos, leave only footprints.
    2. Protect fragile places. No geotags where a place is marked fragile.
    3. Respect people and property. Ask before private land, keep it quiet.
    4. Support local. Eat, sleep and buy local when you can.
    5. Share honestly. Your own photos, real prices, true reports.
    6. Be kind. Reviews describe places, not people.
    7. Pass it on fairly. Meet in public, never pay in advance: deals are between you.
